2. Parties, Recitals and Purpose

2.1 Recitals

A. Customer operates a multimodal freight network across Northern Europe
and the United Kingdom and is implementing a new
transportation-management platform known internally as Project Meridian.

B. Supplier is a specialist consultancy in logistics systems
integration, with particular expertise in TMS, customs messaging, and
operational analytics.

C. Customer wishes to engage Supplier to provide programme leadership,
solution architecture, integration design, data migration oversight and
hypercare support for Project Meridian.

D. The Parties intend this Agreement to govern all such services from
the Effective Date, whether or not a particular workstream is later
documented in a separate SOW.

2.2 Purpose

The purpose of this Agreement is to establish a framework under which
Supplier will deliver the Services in a professional manner, on time and
within the agreed Fees, and under which Customer will provide timely
access, decisions and payment.

3. Scope of Services

3.1 Core workstreams

Subject to the terms of this Agreement, Supplier shall provide the
Services described in Schedule A. Without limiting Schedule A, the core
workstreams are:

1.  Programme leadership and PMO, including a named Engagement Lead
    (Ingrid Halvorsen) at 0.4 FTE and a named Project Manager (Lars
    Ødegård) at 0.8 FTE.

2.  Target architecture for the Meridian TMS stack, covering order
    capture, rating, planning, execution, settlement and customs.

3.  Integration design for SAP S/4HANA (finance), Manhattan WMS, and the
    Customer-facing portal known as Helios Connect.

4.  Data migration strategy, mapping of 14 source systems, dual-run plan
    and cutover runbook.

5.  Test strategy, UAT facilitation and defect triage during the 8-week
    test window commencing 2 June 2025.

6.  Hypercare for eight (8) weeks following Go-Live, targeted for 15
    September 2025, with on-site presence in London during the first two
    weeks.

3.2 Out of scope

Unless expressly added by Change Order, the following are out of scope:
software licence fees payable to third-party vendors; hardware; Customer
internal change-management communications beyond two town-hall
briefings; and legal advice on customs classification.

3.3 Customer dependencies

Customer shall: (a) appoint James Whitaker as executive sponsor and Maya
Chen as day-to-day counterpart; (b) provide access to systems and
subject-matter experts within five (5) Business Days of a written
request; (c) supply source data extracts in the formats specified in
Schedule A by 31 March 2025; and (d) make decisions on open architecture
questions within ten (10) Business Days.

3.4 Location and working pattern

Services will be delivered in a hybrid model: two days per week on
Customer premises at 14 Finsbury Square, London, and three days remotely
from Oslo or other Supplier locations in the EEA. Travel to Customer
sites outside London requires prior written approval and is chargeable
in accordance with Schedule B.

5. Term, Renewal and Termination

5.1 Initial Term

This Agreement commences on the Effective Date (1 January 2025) and
continues for twenty-four (24) months, expiring on 31 December 2026,
unless terminated earlier in accordance with this Clause 5.

5.2 Renewal

Unless either Party gives written notice of non-renewal at least ninety
(90) days before expiry of the then-current Term, this Agreement shall
automatically renew for successive periods of twelve (12) months (each a
“Renewal Term”). For the avoidance of doubt, a later planning slide
circulated by Customer PMO referred to a “36-month partnership horizon”;
that slide is not incorporated and does not extend the contractual Term.

5.3 Termination for convenience

Customer may terminate this Agreement or any SOW for convenience by
giving sixty (60) days’ prior written notice. In that event Customer
shall pay: (a) Fees for Services performed up to the termination date;
(b) non-cancellable third-party costs reasonably incurred; and (c) a
demobilisation fee equal to four (4) weeks of the then-current average
monthly Fees.

5.4 Termination for cause

Either Party may terminate immediately by written notice if the other:
(a) commits a material breach and fails to cure within thirty (30) days
of written notice (or fifteen (15) days in the case of non-payment); (b)
becomes insolvent, enters administration, or ceases to carry on
business; or (c) undergoes a change of control in favour of a direct
competitor of the terminating Party, provided notice is given within
sixty (60) days of the terminating Party becoming aware.

5.5 Consequences of termination

Upon termination or expiry Supplier shall deliver all completed
Deliverables, return or destroy Customer Confidential Information within
thirty (30) days (except one archival copy retained for professional
indemnity purposes for seven (7) years), and invoice for outstanding
Fees. Clauses 6, 7, 8, 9, 11, 12 and 16 survive termination.

6. Confidentiality

6.1 Obligations

Each Receiving Party shall keep Confidential Information in strict
confidence, use it solely to perform this Agreement, and disclose it
only to personnel and subcontractors who have a need to know and are
bound by written confidentiality obligations no less protective than
this Clause 6.

6.2 Duration

The obligations in this Clause 6 survive for three (3) years after
expiry or termination of this Agreement. Trade secrets shall be
protected for so long as they remain trade secrets under applicable law.

Note for reviewers: Schedule E (Data Processing) states that
confidentiality obligations in respect of Personal Data last for five
(5) years after the end of processing. That longer period applies only
to Personal Data and does not amend the three-year period for other
Confidential Information. An earlier draft circulating internally stated
a uniform five-year confidentiality term; that draft was superseded.

6.3 Exceptions

Confidential Information does not include information that: (a) is or
becomes public other than by breach; (b) was rightfully known to the
Receiving Party before disclosure; (c) is independently developed
without use of the Disclosing Party’s information; or (d) is required to
be disclosed by law, provided the Receiving Party gives prompt notice
where legally permitted.

7.1 Background IP

Supplier hereby grants Customer a non-exclusive, perpetual, irrevocable,
worldwide, royalty-free licence to use Supplier Background IP solely as

unless listed in the relevant SOW or approved in a Change Order.
Open-source software may be used if it is licensed under a permissive
licence (MIT, BSD, Apache-2.0) and identified in the Deliverable
documentation.

8. Liability, Indemnities and Insurance

8.1 Cap on liability

Subject to Clause 8.3, each Party’s aggregate liability arising out of
or in connection with this Agreement, whether in contract, tort
(including negligence) or otherwise, shall not exceed the total Fees
paid or payable by Customer to Supplier in the twelve (12) months
preceding the first event giving rise to the claim, or EUR 250,000,
whichever is higher.

8.2 Exclusion of consequential loss

Neither Party shall be liable for loss of profit, loss of revenue, loss
of goodwill, loss of data (except the reasonable cost of
reconstitution), or any indirect or consequential loss, whether or not
foreseeable.

8.3 Unlimited liabilities

Nothing in this Agreement limits liability for: death or personal injury
caused by negligence; fraud or fraudulent misrepresentation; wilful
misconduct; breach of Clause 6 (Confidentiality) to the extent arising
from unauthorised disclosure; or any liability that cannot be limited
under applicable law.

8.4 Indemnities

Supplier shall indemnify Customer against third-party claims that a
Deliverable infringes IP rights in the EEA or the United Kingdom,
provided Customer gives prompt notice and sole control of the defence.
Customer shall indemnify Supplier against claims arising from Customer
data, Customer-provided materials, or Customer’s misuse of Deliverables.

8.5 Insurance

During the Term and for two (2) years thereafter, Supplier shall
maintain: professional indemnity insurance of not less than EUR
2,000,000 per claim; public liability insurance of not less than EUR
5,000,000; and cyber liability insurance of not less than EUR 1,000,000.
Certificates shall be provided within ten (10) Business Days of written
request.

9. Data Protection and Security

Each Party shall comply with applicable data protection law, including
the GDPR and the UK GDPR. Where Supplier processes Personal Data on
behalf of Customer, the Parties shall execute the data processing
addendum in Schedule E before any such processing begins.

Supplier’s primary processing locations are Oslo, Norway and a backup
region in Frankfurt am Main, Germany. No Personal Data shall be
transferred to the United States without Customer’s prior written
consent and an appropriate transfer mechanism (EU Standard Contractual
Clauses, Module 2).

Security measures include: encryption in transit (TLS 1.2 or higher) and
at rest (AES-256); multi-factor authentication for all production
access; logging retained for 180 days; and a documented
incident-response plan. Supplier shall notify Customer of a Personal
Data Breach without undue delay and in any event within seventy-two (72)
hours of becoming aware.

Customer remains the controller of all Personal Data in source extracts.
The categories expected to be processed are: names and business contact
details of Customer employees and contractors; shipment reference
numbers; and, in limited test data only, pseudonymised consignee
postcodes. No special-category data is intended to be processed.

Schedule E — Data Processing Summary

This summary is not a substitute for a full Article 28 DPA. The Parties
shall execute a full DPA before processing begins. Until then, Supplier
shall not process Personal Data other than business contact data of
project participants.

Customer is controller. Supplier is processor for project-related
Personal Data. Sub-processors: Microsoft Ireland (Azure West Europe /
Germany West Central) for document storage; Fjord Analytics AB for
data-quality scripts, subject to a written flow-down.

Processing lasts for the Term plus 90 days for wind-down. Thereafter
Personal Data is deleted or returned within 30 days, except archival
copies required by law. Confidentiality obligations in respect of
Personal Data last for five (5) years after the end of processing.

Primary: Norway (EEA adequacy). Backup: Germany. No US transfer without
SCCs and Customer consent, as stated in Clause 9.
